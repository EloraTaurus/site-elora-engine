How to Read This Record

Records are organised by capability and protocol. Each milestone retains its implementation, review and disclosure dates. New entries join the relevant register; revision dates do not assign earlier disclosure dates to later work.

The archive keeps four questions separate: when a mechanism was first implemented, when it was first located in public disclosure, when it was later examined, and what exists in the current repository. A later examination does not become the origin date of the mechanism it tests.

Established Foundations, Elora Implementation

Identity and access management, least privilege, deterministic policy evaluation, cryptographic hashing, audit logging, provenance, transaction boundaries, current-state validation and TOCTOU analysis are established engineering foundations. This record documents Elora's particular implementation and assembly of those foundations across proposal-to-commit Governance, Standing, Governance Provenance, Replay, Decisions, Observer, Threat Intelligence, WorkerHost/Fabric, Workspace, Sense and evidence records.

The archive records implementation chronology and evidence boundaries. It does not make novelty, exclusivity, patentability, ownership, infringement or certification claims.

Development Methodology

Elora has been independently developed as a solo research project since January 2026. Development uses extensive AI-assisted engineering workflows, including OpenAI Codex, for implementation, refactoring, investigation, documentation and testing support.

AI assistance is an implementation and development workflow, not architectural authority. Architectural direction, integration decisions, implementation review, testing and acceptance of generated or modified implementation remain operator-directed. Accepted changes are retained through normal history in the project's private Git repository.

Public chronology is represented through short commit fingerprints, changelogs, technical disclosures and selected public evidence. Codex token counts, chat counts, activity statistics and screenshots are not used as evidence of authorship or technical provenance. They may corroborate sustained development activity, but they do not establish when or how any specific Elora mechanism was implemented.
