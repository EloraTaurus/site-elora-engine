Guardrail Defence

Separate the Defence Layers

A single pass rate hides where protection came from. Elora records the contribution of each layer and preserves unevaluated states.

Model Adherence

Whether the model held its expected boundary without a downstream governance intervention.

Elora Interception

Whether Elora detected and blocked an unsafe proposal after the model boundary was challenged or breached.

No-Escape Performance

Whether the combined governed environment prevented an unsafe outcome from reaching a consequence-bearing surface.

Combined Defence

An environment-level view that retains the difference between model-held, intercepted, escaped, and governance-not-exercised outcomes.

Evidence and Comparison

Qualified Context

Comparisons retain model, scenario, attack vector, file type, storage method, and available governance conditions.

Bounded Standings

Results can support standings inside a declared test environment without being presented as universal safety rankings.

Reports and Exports

Layered outcomes, evidence references, test coverage, and incomplete states can be reviewed in human-readable and structured forms.

Threat Intelligence Link

Relevant findings can inform governed threat Cases while preserving the distinction between experimental defence evidence and live operational intelligence.

Claim Boundary

Guardrail Defence does not certify a model as universally safe. It reports evidence from declared models, scenarios, tooling, and governance conditions, including failure and non-exercised states.
