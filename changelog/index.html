August 2026 Research Evidence Milestones

  • Observer reports now lead with a Research Verdict before Reasoning and Comparison and the complete Forensic Evidence record.
  • A claim ladder separates recorded, derived, interpreted, provisionally supported, and independently validated evidence.
  • Evidence passports connect summary claims to validity state, comparison cohorts, limitations, and exact run steps.
  • Comparable-answer measurements are coverage-gated and exclude missing, failed, empty, blocked, or refusal outcomes with explicit reasons.
  • Token and runtime reductions are qualified measurements or proxies, not claims about answer quality, direct compute, energy, or causal safety improvement.
  • Bounded Review Packets support outside human and AI orientation without copying prompts, responses, protected methods, or the complete forensic payload.
  • Session Behaviour Trajectories preserve ordered across-turn evidence without presenting sequence as proof of intent, causation, escalation, circumvention, or compromise.
  • Report Builder and dense Threat Intelligence views now use governed managed execution and terminal-memory cleanup.
  • Model Exams, Operator Training, NNLSL candidate processing, and Echo Frequency Learning now connect learning evidence to Runtime Memory Governance.

0.2.7 Foundation and Continuing Highlights

  • Threat Cases now provide interactive reports across attack maps, runtime timelines, evidence, controls, related records, and append-only case activity.
  • Threat recommendations can be compared with the final Governance evaluation, disposition, runtime action, and commit reason.
  • Authorization traces distinguish who initiated governed work from the authority and permissions used to decide commit.
  • Residual-risk acceptance is time-bounded, revocable, append-only, and unable to retroactively approve a runtime decision.
  • Assurance mappings show evidence-present, partial, and not-evidenced states without claiming certification or legal compliance.
  • Threat Intelligence now turns bounded detections into evidence-backed findings and governed investigations.
  • Governance Sessions group related detections while retaining each finding and atomic observation as separately auditable evidence.
  • Threat Findings can link to canonical Elora Decisions and Replay evidence without copying or rewriting those records.
  • Guardrail Defence scorecards separate model adherence, Elora interception, and escaped outcomes under comparable runtime environments.
  • Threat learning and investigations remain evidence-only: they cannot authorize execution, activate detectors, or override Governance.
  • Elora Decisions now provides a categorized, reviewable history of operational decisions rather than isolated notifications.
  • Preparation, intervention, completion, cancellation, failure, and cleanup updates can now be reviewed as one chronological decision story.
  • Observer Research outcomes are summarized in one bounded decision record with completed work, intervention conditions, and repair results.
  • Runtime memory lifecycle visibility now distinguishes active work, pending reclaim, and completed cleanup evidence more clearly.
  • Governed NNLSL education and deterministic lexical synthesis now expose explicit influence, learning-gap, memory, and CPU evidence.
  • Data-driven lexicalization remains disabled and shadow-only while its educational evidence and authority boundaries are evaluated.
  • Inference Governor visibility now explains public runtime decisions through observe, reason, recommend, and govern framing.
  • Public dashboard sections were hardened toward restored-snapshot and consumer-first behaviour rather than heavy request-time rebuilds.
  • Runtime Governance panels and recent decision reports now present reviewer-friendly interpreted evidence instead of telemetry-first raw presentation.
  • Training and Education worker visibility expanded for symbolic communication learning progression.
  • ERIS runtime-intelligence surfaces expanded for runtime posture, worker state, and learning visibility.
  • Historic research reporting is now surfaced through preserved archive paths while rebuilt database-backed surfaces are restored.
  • Continuity-learning and public learning-momentum visibility expanded across runtime and public-safe surfaces.
  • Curiosity governance controls strengthened with split-authority, approval-first, and preparation-flow safeguards.
  • Symbolic interaction provenance and non-neural-first policy telemetry improved auditability of Elora responses.
  • Operator communication controls were tightened so governed symbolic interaction stays bounded and avoids legacy fallback behaviour.
  • Governance capability remains bounded by proposal-to-commit controls, replay evidence, and operator authority.
