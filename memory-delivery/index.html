Reading a Memory Delivery result

Inspect the treatment revision, selected source evidence, admitted and submitted request, budget outcomes and scoring dimensions. Format/reference scores and exact-field content assessment answer different questions. A historical run retains its original protocol; a newer source revision needs its own validation.

This is an experimental comparison mechanism, separate from production learning and from the Adaptive Working Knowledge selector. The implementation and its bounded local validation are documented as E111 in Engineering. The separately published reports carry their own results and limitations.
