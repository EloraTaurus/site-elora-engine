## Reading Rules

- `Earliest implementation evidence` identifies the earliest located private implementation or historical code evidence. It is not automatically a public disclosure date.
- `Earliest located public disclosure` identifies the earliest public-safe source located during this review. Earlier public evidence may exist but is not inferred.
- `Later formal demonstration` reports only the examined path, build, scope and evidence retained by that record.
- `Current implementation` describes current repository behaviour and must not be projected backward without dated evidence.
- Elora's own tests, reports and Public Reports are project evidence, not external or independent validation.

## Known Evidence Gaps

- The five early short fingerprints listed above are retained historical register references but do not resolve in the current checkout. Their full private-history verification therefore remains outside this public-site update.
- The current Public Reports catalogue contains multiple Standing records. Some older projections do not retain complete run-start, completion or generation dates; those dates remain `not established` rather than being inferred from filenames, filesystem times or later publication.
- Preparation-era examination status documentation still says that no formal case had run or labels the examination as proposed. Later frozen artifacts, changelogs and retained records supersede those status phrases without changing the frozen proposition or historical results.
- No source inspected establishes system-wide Standing enforcement, every consequence-bearing path, external authority integration, transition atomicity or independent certification.
