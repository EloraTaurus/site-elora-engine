# Standing and Current Authority Re-evaluation

Standing is Elora's bounded determination that the actor, authority, resource and current governing facts still permit a particular consequence at the protected boundary. The later Standing Examination did not originate this behaviour; it examined a narrower proposition against mechanisms whose current-authority lineage was already implemented.

## Current Repository Flow

For the scoped Document Review path, the current implementation is approximately:

Actor / authenticated operator
-> organisation and session context where applicable
-> scoped permission and capability authority
-> governed document-review proposal
-> current permission, owner, content and classification recheck
-> standing preserved, defeated or unknown
-> response consequence released or held

This flow is not mathematical identity derivation. Human/operator identity, organisation context, permissions, runtime/Tape identity, provenance identity, and model/provider identity remain different evidence domains.

## Paired Proposition and Falsifier

The frozen examination asks whether the examined build derives present standing from changed authoritative classification facts for one private Workspace owner-release consequence:

- a standing-preserving change must preserve the scoped release;
- a standing-defeating change must defeat it;
- the Engine must derive present standing rather than inherit the T0 answer or require an upstream oracle to precompute it.

The falsifier covers inherited T0 standing after a defeating change, treating a preserving change as defeating, or requiring the upstream source to provide the conclusion. Shared invariants require the same frozen definition, nominal permission, scoped consequence, source/evidence contracts and build-bound execution evidence across the pair.

## Retained Result and Claim Ceiling

The canonical Public Reports collection contains multiple retained Standing Examination v1 records, including evidence-insufficient runs and bounded passed records. The dated `2026-09-25` record reports that the frozen examination was satisfied for that build and paired scope only. It also retains `stop_required: true`; the result must be read with that stop condition and its claim ceiling rather than as a general release or certification signal.

That result does not establish:

- system-wide governability;
- identical enforcement on every consequence-bearing path;
- external identity or authority-provider integration;
- transition atomicity outside the recorded transaction and boundary evidence;
- an operating envelope under arbitrary load or timing pressure;
- independent certification or external validation.

The record's own ceiling remains controlling: satisfaction applies only to the examined build and paired scope, and governability does not propagate.
