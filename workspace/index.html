Governed Review

Document and Webpage Tasks

Authorised text, Markdown and bounded webpage review experiments retain explicit task initiation, context preparation, model compatibility, provider dispatch, completion, approval and release evidence.

Persistent Follow-Up

Workspace can continue a review against the retained authorised context while preserving task scope and provenance. Original material remains private unless separately permitted for disclosure.

Comparisons and Charts

Model-free comparison building, grouped or stacked charts, tooltips and image export support structured document analysis without requiring every presentation operation to become model inference.

Clear Holds

Unavailable authority, failed admission, incomplete output, policy denial and withheld release remain distinct outcomes with bounded explanations.

Context, Recall and Thinking Research

Adaptive Context

Context requests are compared with actual admission, model capability evidence and shared resource limits. A requested size does not prove that the provider exercised it.

Conversation Recall

Temporary session memory, compaction and fact tracing expose what was selected and whether bounded evidence survived final fitting and transport submission. Correct output alone does not prove exclusive recall.

Thinking Profiles

Light, Medium and Heavy profiles are advisory allocations within one physical context. Thinking and answer previews are separated, but exact channel token use is not inferred when the provider does not report it.

Benchmarks

Context Window, Thinking/Response and effort comparisons retain run-identified exports and previous results. Rankings require operator interpretation and do not establish a universal quality benefit.

Authority and Delivery

Current Authority

Scoped Document Review research rechecks current standing at protected boundaries. An unavailable lookup is not converted into permission, and historical assignment is not reused as fresh evidence.

Content Binding

Permission and the binding between an authorised subject and reviewed content are evaluated separately. Changed or unavailable content may prevent release even when permission remains allowed.

Governed Streaming

Answer content can remain internal until completed-response commit evaluation. Progress visibility does not imply that an answer has been authorised for release.

Research Boundary

Local controlled scenarios are evidence of implemented checks, not proof of external identity integration, universal enforcement, atomicity, or deployed-provider behaviour.
